## How to Read This Agreement

Each row maps an approved community college (CC) course to the Sac State Computer
Science course it satisfies. Only courses listed here transfer directly into the CS
major. A final transfer evaluation is confirmed by Academic Advising.

## Courses That Do Not Transfer Directly

Introductory CC courses such as "CIS 1 — Introduction to Computers" do not transfer
into the CS major and do not satisfy any major requirement. They may count toward
general education only.

## Transfer Credit Limits

A maximum of 70 lower-division transfer units may apply to the degree. Upper-division
CS core courses (CSC 130 and above) must be completed at Sac State and cannot be
satisfied by community college coursework.

## Confirmation

Students should bring their CC transcript to Academic Advising for an official
transfer credit evaluation. BullBot's mapping is a guide based on this agreement and
does not replace the official evaluation.
