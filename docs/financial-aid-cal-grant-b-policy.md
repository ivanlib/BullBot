## Overview

Cal Grant B provides a living-allowance stipend and, after the first year, tuition
and fee assistance to eligible low-income California undergraduates. Awards must be
renewed each academic year; renewal is not automatic.

## Renewal Requirements

To renew a Cal Grant B award, a student must meet all of the following:

1. Maintain satisfactory academic progress (SAP) as defined by the campus financial
   aid office, including a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0.
2. Remain enrolled at least half-time in an eligible program.
3. Continue to meet the Cal Grant income and asset ceilings for the award year.
4. File a renewal FAFSA (or California Dream Act Application) by the March 2 priority
   deadline each year.
5. Not have exceeded the maximum award eligibility period (up to four years of
   full-time equivalent benefits).

## Income and Asset Ceilings

Renewal eligibility depends on the family income and asset ceilings published by the
California Student Aid Commission for the award year. Students whose income exceeds
the ceiling for a given year may lose eligibility for that year.

## Loss and Reinstatement

A student who drops below half-time enrollment or below the minimum GPA may have the
award suspended. Reinstatement may be possible after the student returns to good
standing; the Financial Aid & Scholarships Office determines reinstatement.

## Where to Confirm

Specific income ceilings, GPA calculations, and your individual renewal status are
confirmed only by the Financial Aid & Scholarships Office. BullBot does not have
access to your student record and cannot confirm your personal eligibility.
