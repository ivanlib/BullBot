## Overview

The Data Science minor requires 18 units of coursework and is open to students in any
major, including Computer Science. A minimum GPA of 2.0 in minor coursework is
required. Some courses overlap with CS technical electives, which lets CS majors add
the minor without significantly extending time to graduation.

## Required Courses

- STAT 50 — Introduction to Probability and Statistics (3 units)
- CSC 177 — Data Mining and Analytics (3 units)
- STAT 115A — Statistical Computing (3 units)

## Elective Courses

Choose 9 additional units from the following approved list:

- CSC 180 — Intelligent Systems (3 units)
- CSC 211 — Machine Learning (3 units)
- STAT 116 — Applied Regression Analysis (3 units)
- MATH 102 — Linear Algebra for Data Science (3 units)

## Overlap With Computer Science

CSC 177 and STAT 115A are approved Computer Science technical electives (see the CS
catalog, Technical Electives section). A CS major who selects these as technical
electives applies the units to both the major and the minor, reducing the extra units
needed for the minor to as few as 9.

## Planning Note

To add the Data Science minor without delaying graduation, students should take STAT
50 early (it is a prerequisite for several electives) and schedule CSC 177 and STAT
115A in terms where they also count as CS technical electives. A final plan must be
confirmed with Academic Advising.
