## Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (CS) at California State University,
Sacramento requires 120 total units, including lower-division preparation,
upper-division core courses, technical electives, and university general education.
A minimum overall GPA of 2.0 and a minimum GPA of 2.0 in the major are required to
graduate.

## Lower-Division Requirements

Students must complete the following lower-division courses before advancing to
upper-division CS coursework:

- CSC 20 — Programming Concepts and Methodology I (3 units)
- CSC 28 — Programming Concepts and Methodology II (3 units)
- CSC 35 — Introduction to Computer Architecture (3 units)
- MATH 30 — Calculus I (4 units)
- MATH 31 — Calculus II (4 units)
- MATH 35 — Introduction to Linear Algebra (3 units)

Transfer students should complete as many of these as possible at their community
college. See the articulation agreement for approved equivalents.

## Upper-Division Core Requirements

- CSC 130 — Data Structures and Algorithm Analysis (3 units)
- CSC 131 — Software Engineering (3 units)
- CSC 133 — Object-Oriented Computer Graphics Programming (3 units)
- CSC 134 — Embedded Systems (3 units)
- CSC 135 — Computer Organization (3 units)
- CSC 138 — Computer Networks (3 units)
- CSC 139 — Operating System Principles (3 units)
- CSC 140 — Advanced Algorithms and Data Structures (3 units)

## Technical Electives

Students choose 12 units of upper-division CSC technical electives. CSC 177
(Data Mining and Analytics) and STAT 115A are approved electives that also count
toward the Data Science minor (see the Data Science minor requirements document).

## Degree Roadmap

A typical four-year roadmap places lower-division math and CSC 20/28 in the first
year, CSC 35 and CSC 130/131 in the second year, upper-division core in the third
year, and technical electives plus the senior project in the fourth year. Transfer
students entering with lower-division preparation complete the upper-division core
and electives over four to five semesters.

To avoid delaying graduation, students adding a minor should overlap minor courses
with approved technical electives wherever the catalog allows.

## Graduation Requirements

- 120 total units
- Minimum 2.0 overall GPA and 2.0 major GPA
- Completion of all core and the required number of elective units
- Final degree evaluation confirmed by Academic Advising
